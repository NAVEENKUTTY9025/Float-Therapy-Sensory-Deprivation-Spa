What is floating

An hour where nothing is asked of you.

You lie back in a shallow pool of skin-temperature water saturated with 500kg of pharmaceutical-grade Epsom salt. The water supports you completely — no pressure, no gravity, no sound.

Lights off. Sound off. Distraction off. What's left is your own internal rhythm — and the space to finally hear it.

  • Zero-gravity weightlessness removes all pressure from joints and muscles
  • Sensory input is reduced to near-zero, giving your nervous system a break
  • Magnesium-rich Epsom salt is absorbed through the skin and relaxes the body
  • Brainwave activity slows into theta state — the same state as deep meditation
The Benefits

Therapy for the mind,
the body, and the noise.

Floating isn't a trend. It's a clinically studied intervention for stress, chronic pain, and cognitive overload — practiced in silence.

Mental Clarity

Reduced sensory input allows the brain to enter theta state — the same brainwave pattern found in deep meditation and pre-sleep. Anxiety drops. Focus returns.

Physical Recovery

Muscular tension releases within minutes. Blood flow improves. Epsom salt reduces inflammation. Athletes use floating to accelerate recovery between training days.

Deep Sleep

Floating triggers a parasympathetic response that lasts for hours after your session. Many clients report the best sleep of their month on float days.

Chronic Pain Relief

The elimination of gravity means no compression of the spine or joints. Those with fibromyalgia, arthritis, and back pain often find lasting relief.

Hormonal Balance

Studies show floating lowers cortisol and adrenaline while raising endorphins and dopamine. The result is a sustained sense of wellbeing.

Meditative Depth

Regular floaters report faster access to meditative states, deeper breathwork, and a stronger ability to sit with discomfort — on and off the mat.

Your visit

What happens when
you arrive.

Every session is designed to slow you down — from the moment you step inside LUMA, to the moment you float out.

01
Arrival

Check in. Slow down.

You're welcomed into our quiet lounge with a cup of herbal tea. Phones stay in the locker. From this point, everything is designed to bring your heart rate down.

02
Preparation

Shower & settle in.

Private changing rooms with showers, robes, and towels. A quick pre-float rinse keeps the tank water pristine and helps your skin absorb the magnesium.

03
The Float

60 or 90 minutes of nothing.

Step into your private float suite. The tank lid is entirely optional. Music fades out after 10 minutes. You lie back. Time disappears.

04
Integration

Return slowly.

After your float, a soft chime signals the end. Post-float shower, then a slow return to the lounge — tea, water, silence. Take as long as you need.
