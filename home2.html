Why float

Different floats,
different gifts.

Each service at LUMA works on a different system. Explore what each one offers.

Mental Clarity

Reduced sensory input allows the brain to enter theta state — the same brainwave pattern found in deep meditation and pre-sleep.

Anxiety drops. Focus returns. The mental chatter that follows you all day finally quiets.

  • Theta brainwave access within 20 minutes
  • Reduced cortisol and adrenaline
  • Improved focus and decision-making

Physical Recovery

Muscular tension releases within minutes. Blood flow improves. Epsom salt reduces inflammation. Athletes use floating to accelerate recovery between training days.

The elimination of gravity means no compression of the spine or joints — the deepest rest your body can experience.

  • Accelerated muscle recovery
  • Reduced inflammation
  • Improved circulation

Deep Sleep

Floating triggers a parasympathetic response that lasts for hours after your session. Many clients report the best sleep of their month on float days.

The combination of magnesium absorption, reduced cortisol, and theta state sets the stage for truly restorative sleep.

  • Improved sleep onset
  • Deeper REM cycles
  • Reduced insomnia symptoms

Chronic Pain Relief

The elimination of gravity means no compression of the spine or joints. Those with fibromyalgia, arthritis, and back pain often find lasting relief.

Magnesium absorption adds a secondary layer of relief by relaxing muscles and reducing inflammation.

  • Zero spinal compression
  • Reduced fibromyalgia symptoms
  • Relief from arthritis and back pain

Hormonal Balance

Studies show floating lowers cortisol and adrenaline while raising endorphins and dopamine. The result is a sustained sense of wellbeing.

Regular floaters report improved mood, reduced anxiety, and a greater capacity for joy and connection.

  • Lower cortisol and adrenaline
  • Higher endorphins and dopamine
  • Improved mood and emotional resilience
Your visit

A timeline of
letting go.

Every float follows the same gentle arc — from arrival to integration. Here's how your visit unfolds.

00:00 — Arrival

Check in. Slow down.

You're welcomed into our quiet lounge with a cup of herbal tea. Phones stay in the locker. From this point, everything is designed to bring your heart rate down.

00:10 — Preparation

Shower & settle in.

Private changing rooms with showers, robes, and towels. A quick pre-float rinse keeps the tank water pristine and helps your skin absorb the magnesium.

00:20 — The Float

60 or 90 minutes of nothing.

Step into your private float suite. The tank lid is entirely optional. Music fades out after 10 minutes. You lie back. Time disappears.

01:50 — Integration

Return slowly.

After your float, a soft chime signals the end. Post-float shower, then a slow return to the lounge — tea, water, silence. Take as long as you need.
