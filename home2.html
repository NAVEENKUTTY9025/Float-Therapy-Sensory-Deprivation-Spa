Your visit

A timeline of
letting go.

Every float follows the same gentle arc — from arrival to integration. Here's how your visit unfolds.

00:00 — Arrival

Check in. Slow down.

You're welcomed into our quiet lounge with a cup of herbal tea. Phones stay in the locker. From this point, everything is designed to bring your heart rate down.

00:10 — Preparation

Shower & settle in.

Private changing rooms with showers, robes, and towels. A quick pre-float rinse keeps the tank water pristine and helps your skin absorb the magnesium.

00:20 — The Float

60 or 90 minutes of nothing.

Step into your private float suite. The tank lid is entirely optional. Music fades out after 10 minutes. You lie back. Time disappears.

Return slowly.

After your float, a soft chime signals the end. Post-float shower, then a slow return to the lounge — tea, water, silence. Take as long as you need.

Got Questions?

First time floating?
You are completely safe.

Sensory deprivation is gentle and completely within your control. Here are the top answers for beginners.

You are in complete control of your experience at all times. Our tanks and pods do not latch or lock; you can open the door with a fingertip or leave it completely propped open. We also offer Open Float Suites with 8-foot high ceilings and no enclosure whatsoever. You can leave ambient starlight lights on or listen to calming audio as long as you wish.
Falling asleep is common and completely safe! With over 1,000 lbs of dissolved medical-grade Epsom salt, the water is dense enough (1.28 specific gravity) that your body remains effortlessly buoyant on top of the water without any physical effort. You cannot roll over accidentally, and even if salt water splashes near your eyes, it wakes you immediately.
Our float water is cleaner than drinking water. Between every single client, 100% of the water undergoes a rigorous 4-step purification cycle: high-output germicidal UV light, ozone sanitation, medical hydrogen peroxide infusion, and 1-micron mechanical filtration. Furthermore, the extreme salt concentration creates an environment where bacteria and microbes cannot survive.
Just yourself! We provide everything: luxurious organic shampoo, conditioner, body wash, makeup remover, earplugs, towels, robes, and hairdryers. Most floaters prefer floating without swimwear because swimsuits can press against skin and create tactile sensation, but swimwear is completely welcomed if that makes you feel most comfortable.
