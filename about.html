About LUMA

Where silence
becomes medicine.

LUMA was born from a simple belief: the modern mind is overloaded, and the deepest healing happens when we remove everything. Zero gravity. Zero noise. Zero expectation.

Established
2023 · Copenhagen
Our story

Built by floaters,
for floaters.

We opened LUMA after years of struggling with our own nervous systems — burnout, chronic pain, and the endless hum of a connected world. Floating was the only thing that truly quieted it.

So we built a space dedicated to that quiet. A sensory deprivation spa where the water is skin-temperature, the Epsom salt is pharmaceutical-grade, and the silence is protected at all costs.

  • 500kg of Epsom salt per tank — the highest density in the region
  • Custom-built float suites with full light and sound isolation
  • Water filtered and UV-sterilized between every session
  • A team trained in trauma-informed care and breathwork
Our Philosophy

Less noise. More you.

We believe the body knows how to heal — it just needs the space. Floating creates that space by removing gravity, sound, light, and touch.

The Space

Designed to disappear.

Every corner of LUMA is built to reduce cognitive load — from the warm mineral tones to the absence of mirrors in the lounge.

Float Suite
Private · Soundproof · Lightproof

A pause in the architecture of your day.

The lounge is kept intentionally dim. The tea is herbal. Phones are left at the door. We don't rush you in or out — your session includes time to arrive and time to return.

Each float suite is entirely private, with its own shower, changing area, and tank. You control the lid, the light, and the music. Everything else is optional.
