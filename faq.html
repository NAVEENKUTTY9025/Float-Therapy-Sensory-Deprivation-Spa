Your first visit includes a 15-minute orientation where a float guide walks you through everything. You'll shower, step into your private suite, and lie back in the water. The tank lid is entirely optional — you can leave it open the whole time. Most first-timers find the first 10 minutes are about settling in, and the rest is pure stillness. You are always in control.
Claustrophobia is common, which is why the tank lid is never closed unless you want it to be. You can float with the lid fully open, partially open, or closed. You can also get out at any time — the door is never locked. Many people find that once they're floating, the feeling of spaciousness in zero gravity actually eases anxiety. If you do feel uncomfortable, simply sit up and take a breath. Our team is nearby if you need anything.
Just yourself. We provide towels, robes, earplugs, shower amenities, and post-float tea. We recommend avoiding caffeine for a few hours before your float and not shaving immediately before (the salt water can sting freshly shaved skin). If you have long hair, we provide hair ties. Contact lenses should be removed before floating.
The tank contains about 10 inches of water saturated with 500kg of pharmaceutical-grade Epsom salt (magnesium sulfate). This creates a solution so dense that you float effortlessly on the surface — no muscle tension required. The water is heated to 35.0°C, which is skin-receptor neutral, meaning your body can't tell where it ends and the water begins. Combined with the elimination of light, sound, and gravity, your nervous system enters a deep state of rest.
Floatation therapy (also called REST — Restricted Environmental Stimulation Therapy) has been studied for decades. Research shows it can:
  • Reduce cortisol and adrenaline levels
  • Increase dopamine and endorphins
  • Lower blood pressure and heart rate
  • Improve sleep quality and reduce insomnia
  • Reduce chronic pain, especially fibromyalgia and back pain
  • Support meditation depth and creativity
It's used by athletes, artists, therapists, and anyone looking to reset their nervous system.
Theta brainwaves (4–8 Hz) are associated with deep meditation, REM sleep, and the hypnagogic state between wakefulness and sleep. In the tank, with no external sensory input, your brain naturally slows into theta within about 20 minutes. This is why floaters often describe vivid imagery, sudden clarity, or a profound sense of calm. It's the same brainwave state experienced by advanced meditators.
Our standard sessions are 60 or 90 minutes in the tank. We recommend 90 minutes for the full experience — the first 20–30 minutes are often spent settling in, and the deepest states come after. Your appointment also includes time before and after for showering, changing, and lounging. Most clients spend about 2 hours total with us.

The high salt concentration makes the water naturally hostile to bacteria, but we go further: the water is fully filtered three times between every session, treated with UV sterilization, and tested for pH and sanitizer levels multiple times daily. We follow the same standards as medical spa facilities. You can request a water quality report at any time.
Floating is safe for most people, but we recommend consulting a doctor if you have:
  • Open wounds, recent tattoos, or skin infections
  • Epilepsy or seizure disorders
  • Severe claustrophobia or psychosis
  • Incontinence or certain kidney conditions
  • Recently dyed hair (color can bleed into the water)
If you're unsure, just ask. We'd rather you skip a session than have a bad experience.
