Common Questions

Before you book.

When am I charged?

Single floats are charged at the time of booking. Membership charges recur monthly on the date you joined. You can pause or cancel anytime from your client dashboard.

What's the cancellation policy?

We ask for 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule without charge. Within 24 hours, 50% of the session fee applies. No-shows are charged in full. If you're sick, just call us.

Can I roll over unused floats?

The Reset members can roll over up to 2 unused floats per month. Rolled floats expire after 60 days. Single sessions cannot be rolled over but can be gifted or transferred.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes — for single floats, packages, and memberships. Gift cards never expire and can be booked at the recipient's convenience. Available at the front desk or by contacting us.

Are there student or senior discounts?

We offer a 15% discount for students with valid ID, and a 10% discount for seniors 65+. Discounts apply to single floats and cannot be combined with other offers.

Can I bring a friend?

Each float suite is private, but you can book multiple suites for the same time slot. The Reset members receive a free quarterly guest pass. Private group bookings start at $450.
